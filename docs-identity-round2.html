Voice assistant identity — round 2

Round 1 tried to invent a glyph. The references say that is the hardest route and the one that almost nobody wins. This round starts from what the successful assistant identities actually do on Mobbin, sorts them into four routes, and builds five directions on those routes instead.

What the references actually do

Twelve apps, four routes. Links open the Mobbin screen.

Route 1 · Colour, no glyph

Meta AI is a hollow gradient ring floating above WhatsApp's tab bar. No symbol inside it. It is the same ring in Instagram and Messenger, so the identity is the colour and the shape, and it never competes with the host app's green or purple.

WhatsApp · Meta AIWhatsApp · empty stateChatGPT · Voice

Lesson: the cross-app problem is solved by giving the assistant its own fixed colour and a hollow form, not by drawing a better icon.

Route 2 · Light and material

Alexa is a blue light along the bottom edge, the Echo ring flattened onto a phone. ChatGPT Voice is a soft blue cloud in a circle. Neither has a drawable icon. The identity is a glow, and the glow is also the speaking state.

Alexa · listening barChatGPT · voice orbPerplexity · dot-matrix wave

Lesson: when the product is a voice, the mark can be the sound made visible. Perplexity's halftone dots are a texture you recognise before you read anything.

Route 3 · A named object

Spotify's DJ is a green disc with a thin ring and two letters, sitting among content tiles as if it were an album. Snapchat's My AI is a contact in the chat list. Both make the assistant a thing with a name, not a control.

Spotify · DJ tileSnapchat · My AICRED · concierge

Lesson: two letters in a circle beat any abstract glyph once the name is known. The name does the work; the mark just holds it.

Route 4 · Character

Notion AI is a hand-drawn monochrome face, and when you pick a persona it gets a hat. Duolingo's Lily is a full character you video-call. Faces make the assistant approachable, and they set the expectation that it talks back.

Notion AI · faceNotion · persona hatDuolingo · Lily

Lesson: a face is the strongest approachability cue and the biggest promise. Right for a conversational assistant, early for one that narrates.

A · Ring recommendedRoute 1

A hollow ring in a fixed indigo → violet → mint sweep. Nothing inside it. Indigo and mint are already the brand's two colours; the violet between them is the assistant's own note. It is the Meta AI move, and it solves the Swap / BaaS / GoSmart problem the way Meta solved WhatsApp / Instagram / Messenger: the ring never changes, the host does.

Hollow matters. Every other button on these screens is a filled disc. A ring is the one shape that reads as "different kind of thing" at 40 px without needing a symbol. Monochrome fallback is a plain hollow ring, still distinct from filled buttons.

B · Halo Route 2

A soft gradient disc with a white core, like a light seen through frosted glass. At rest it is a quiet glow on the card. When the assistant speaks, the same gradient appears as a thin light bar along the bottom edge of the sheet, Alexa-style, so the whole screen "has a voice" and not just one button.

Risk: glows are hard to draw at 14 px and impossible in monochrome. This is an identity for the moment of speaking more than for a tab icon.

D · Dot-wave Route 2

A waveform built from dots instead of bars, Perplexity's halftone made into a button. The texture is the identity: no other app in a rider's phone uses stippled sound. It also gives the player pill a native language, since the waveform in the sheet can be drawn from the same dots.

Risk: at 14 px the dots merge into a smudge. Needs a bar fallback for tiny sizes, which starts to undo the point.

What I'd take to Adil

A, the ring. It is the only direction that answers the cross-app brief with a precedent riders already have on their phones, and it costs nothing to learn: it looks like the Meta AI button, and every rider with WhatsApp already knows that shape means "the smart thing". It has no glyph to get wrong, it survives monochrome, and it grows: empty today, a letter when the assistant has a name, a glowing core when it speaks.

Bring C beside it as the answer to "what goes inside once we name her". Keep E in the drawer for the conversational version; a battery with a face is the most Battery Smart thing on this page, and it deserves a product that can listen.

If the room wants a symbol regardless, D is the one to develop, not anything from round 1.
